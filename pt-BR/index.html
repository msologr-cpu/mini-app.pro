What is a Telegram mini-app?

Telegram mini-apps are web applications that launch directly in Telegram. They open instantly, require no installation and work on any device — a convenient way to combine sales, education, support and content in a single interface.

What you can build inside a mini-app

  • User accounts
  • CRM with segmentation and tags
  • Service and product catalogs
  • Courses and lessons
  • Subscriptions and monetisation
  • Referral programs
  • Forms, surveys and quizzes
  • Message automation
  • Analytics and statistics
  • Chat and operator support

Benefits of mini-apps

Mini-apps work everywhere Telegram is available. No need to download an app, set up hosting, buy a domain or hire developers. All features live inside the bot and updates deploy automatically.

Who mini-apps are for

  • Online schools and education projects
  • Expert and creator products
  • Companies and brands
  • Local and service businesses
  • Agencies and marketers
  • Communities and clubs
  • Bloggers and media
