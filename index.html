What is a Telegram Mini App

Telegram Mini Apps are web applications that launch right inside Telegram. They open instantly, require no installation, and run on any device. It's a convenient way to unite sales, education, support, and content in one place.

What you can build in a Mini App

  • User accounts
  • CRM with segmentation and tags
  • Service and product catalogs
  • Courses and lessons
  • Subscriptions and monetization
  • Referral programs
  • Forms, surveys, and quizzes
  • Message automation
  • Analytics and statistics
  • Support chat and operators

Benefits of Mini Apps

Mini Apps work everywhere Telegram is available. There is no need to download apps, configure hosting, buy a domain, or hire developers. All features live inside the bot, and updates roll out automatically.

Who Mini Apps are for

  • Online schools and educational projects
  • Author-driven expert products
  • Companies and brands
  • Local and service businesses
  • Agencies and marketers
  • Communities and clubs
  • Bloggers and media
