What Telegram Mini Apps are and how they work

Telegram Mini Apps are lightweight web apps embedded in the messenger interface. They launch through a bot, are optimized for mobile, and give fast access to registration, learning, payments, quizzes, and communities.

Mini Apps vs. GetCourse: which is better for courses?

The approaches differ: GetCourse is a classic platform with dashboards and integrations; Mini Apps are a native engagement model inside Telegram. Key differences:

Why Telegram Mini Apps beat classic web tech

Pros: no installation, quick access to services, and a wide range of scenarios from registration to gamification. Cons: fewer options than full mobile apps and the need to request device permissions for certain features.
